Artistic Principle

Shot&Prompt is a form of visual creation that begins with a photograph of the real world and unfolds through a sequence of images generated by prompts, thus extending Roland Barthes’s “that-has-been” into the “that-has-been&imagined”. The photograph is no longer treated as an end in itself, but as the seed image of an evolving visual exploration. Each new image retains something from the previous one while introducing a variation, a displacement, or an opening toward other possibilities.

The choices remain human: what to transform, preserve, or reject, and how meaning emerges through the sequence. Generative AI is simply the tool that makes these transformations possible. The result is a visual morphogenesis in which reality gradually opens onto imagination.

From Selfies to Shompties

Today, everyone has a camera on their smartphone, wherever they are and at any moment. Images have become an everyday form of sharing: I was here, I saw this, this happened. The selfie pushes this logic even further by turning the camera toward oneself, while the world is reduced to a background.

Shot&Prompt begins with a simple idea: imagination lives within each of us and finds expression in a unique way of seeing, interpreting, and narrating the world. What is still needed are the tools and a method to give that vision form.

Shot&Prompt therefore offers the shomptie as a counterpoint to the selfie: the photograph is no longer an endpoint, but the starting point of a visual exploration. A shomptie begins with a shot and unfolds through prompts. What is shared is no longer simply the world as it is: it is a way of imagining reality, of seeing it as it could be.

By combining the camera everyone already carries, prompting, and generative AI, the shomptie transforms an encounter with reality into a visual creation. Not just a new way to photograph. A new why.

Narrative

A shomptie that tells a story. Each prompted image moves the action forward, introduces a character, transforms a situation, or creates a turning point.

Poetic

A shomptie in which the photograph becomes poetic material. Reality drifts toward atmosphere, dream, metaphor, memory, or emotion.

Documentary

A shomptie strongly anchored in a real place. Prompted images extend the photograph by revealing the historical, social, hidden, or imagined layers of that place.

Surreal

A shomptie in which the initial photograph gradually drifts toward the strange, the impossible, or the absurd. Reality remains recognizable, but becomes unstable.

Critical

A shomptie that, through its successive transformations, questions a situation: a place, a sign, a rule, an urban trace, a social gesture, or a collective memory.

Offbeat

A shomptie that finds humor in a mismatch, an incongruity, or an unexpected twist. Reality remains familiar, but the situation gradually becomes funny, improbable, or mischievous.

An Open Visual Dialogue

Each shomptie unfolds within a language specific to Shot&Prompt, arising from the interplay between photographed reality, the elements it brings into play, and the successive transformations generated through prompts. This language draws on a lexicon of forms, colours, motifs, textures and objects, and on a grammar that preserves, shifts, combines, repeats, substitutes or metamorphoses them from one image to the next. As these elements evolve from source to conclusion, meaning gradually emerges. Each shomptie thus traces a singular trajectory. Viewers can, in turn, appropriate the codes of this language, play with them, and reshape its meaning.

Shot&Prompt thereby opens up a visual space in which viewers can take part: by starting from their own seed photograph, by returning to a given source photograph to follow a different trajectory, or by extending an existing sequence from its closing image.

Shot&Prompt — from real photograph to prompted visual creation.
